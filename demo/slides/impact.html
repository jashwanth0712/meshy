All numbers come from the repo's own runs. The bar is a real revoke against db1.prod.meshydemo.eth on Sepolia (block 12073043): the unregister() transaction was mined 7.8 s after it was sent, the session was dead at 9.46 s, and meshyd's own reaction from seeing the block to killing the session was 120 ms, read back from the latencyMs attribute of the Arkiv revoke entity. Across three CLI revokes meshyd took 120 to 192 ms; a dashboard revoke by a delegated admin killed the session 1.6 s after the block was mined, with a 216 ms reaction. On an anvil fork of Sepolia with the real ENSv2 contracts and 12 s blocks, five runs gave p50 11.71 s and p95 12.19 s from transaction sent to session dead, under the 15 s target. In other words, nearly all of the time is Ethereum block inclusion, not Meshy. The Linux sandbox suite blocks 9 escape attempts in Docker, the services end-to-end script passes 21 of 21 checks including a live TCP stream cut by an on-chain revoke, 31 Foundry tests pass for the contracts, and the dashboard went from 144 RPC requests and about 40 s to 13 requests and about 10 s.
